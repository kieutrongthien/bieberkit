The admin speaks English, Vietnamese and Chinese (UI-05). `@bieberkit/admin-core` exports the dictionaries, `translator`, `localeOf` and the formats of numbers, dates and money. `@bieberkit/admin-antd` has a language select in the header, keeps the choice in the browser, follows the language of the browser otherwise, and gives Ant Design its locale with the two Vietnamese texts that the library lacks.
