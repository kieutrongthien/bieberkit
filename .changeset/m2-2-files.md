`f.file()` and `f.image()` make a field that holds the id of a media record. A write is refused at the field unless the file exists, is uploaded and is of a type that the field takes, `_meta` draws the field as a file, and the admin sends the file to the storage with a signed URL. The policy of the admin names the origin of the bucket in `connect-src` and `img-src` when the disk is S3. A disk that cannot sign (the local one) cannot take files from the browser.
